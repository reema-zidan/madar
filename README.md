# Madar Tech Landing Page

A responsive Arabic (`RTL`) landing page developed for **Madar Tech (Coworking Space & Coffee)** in Khan Yunis to present the organization's identity, coworking and training services, learning programs, and contact channels.

## Sections & Features
- **Navigation Bar:** Quick links to Home, About Us, Services, Training Programs, and Contact Us, along with a **Book Now** action button.
- **Hero Section:** Introduces Madar Tech as a learning and coworking hub in Khan Yunis with call-to-action buttons (*Explore Services* and *Contact Us*).
- **About Us:** Highlights mission, vision, and bridging academic knowledge with practical experience.
- **Services:** Dedicated cards/sections for Training & Development, Coworking Spaces, Training Halls, and Educational Activities.
- **Training Programs:** Showcases tracks including **Front-End Development**, **Artificial Intelligence**, and **Digital Skills**, each with a brief description and *Learn More* action.
- **Why Madar:** Lists key benefits such as a motivating environment, stable internet, available power, hands-on training, networking, and mentorship.
- **Contact Us & Footer:** Includes a contact form (Name, Email, Phone, Message) configured to send inquiries via **WhatsApp**, paired with official contact details and social links.

## Tech Stack & Visual Identity
- **Markup & Layout:** Semantic HTML5, CSS3 (Flexbox & CSS Grid), Bootstrap, and full Arabic `RTL` layout support.
- **Interactivity:** Vanilla JavaScript for UI interactions such as mobile navigation and buttons.
- **Brand Palette:** Oxford Blue (`#03213D`), Fresh Lime (`#D5E159`), Graphite Gray (`#333333`), and Pale Silver (`#DAD9D9`).
